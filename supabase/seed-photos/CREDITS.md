# Seed photo credits

Demo photos used by `scripts/seed.ts`. Every file below comes from Wikimedia Commons and is tagged
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) on its Commons file page. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . All dates checked: 2026-10-18.

Honest notes:

- Most photos were NOT taken in Thailand (Ukraine, India, Ghana, USA, Nigeria, UK, Australia and others). Only the two flood photos show Thailand. They stand in for typical Thai reports.
- The street-light / power-line photos are loose stand-ins: no CC0 photo of a genuinely broken street light was found. `light-1.jpg` is a rusted garden lamp post and `light-2.jpg` is a power service line to a house.
- The "unsafe spot" photos are broken paving and a broken fence, not open manholes.
- The files were resized (longest side at most 1600 px), re-encoded as JPEG and had their metadata removed. `flood-2.jpg` and `unsafe-1.jpg` are cropped parts of the original images. Nothing was blurred or edited otherwise.
- `tree-2.jpg` is a dash camera frame with a burned-in date and GPS caption from the USA.
